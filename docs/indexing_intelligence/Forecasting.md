# Indexing Growth & Probability Forecasting Model

## Overview
The Forecasting Engine predicts index likelihood, expected crawl delays, and recovery probabilities using historical index outcomes and real-time health data.

## Forecast Metrics
- **Probability Levels**: Very High (>85%), High (70-85%), Medium (55-70%), Low (40-55%), Very Low (<40%).
- **Expected Index Time**: Computed in hours based on domain authority and crawl frequency.
- **Weekly & Monthly Growth Rates**: Predicted percentage increase in indexed backlinks.
- **Recovery Probability**: Estimated likelihood of re-indexing lost backlinks following technical repairs.
