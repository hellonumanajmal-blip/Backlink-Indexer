# Search Visibility Metrics & Mathematical Model

## Metrics
1. **Visibility Score**: $0.85 \times \text{Index Rate} + 0.15 \times (100 - \text{Lost Index Rate})$
2. **Index Rate**: $\frac{\text{Indexed URLs}}{\text{Total Managed URLs}} \times 100\%$
3. **Discovery Rate**: Rate at which newly submitted URLs enter the discovered/crawled state.
4. **Retention Rate**: Percentage of previously indexed URLs that maintain their indexed status over 30 days.
5. **Lost Index Rate**: Percentage of URLs that dropped out of the index.
6. **Recovery Rate**: Rate of successful re-indexing after applying AI recommendations.
