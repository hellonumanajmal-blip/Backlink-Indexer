# Phase 25 Link Opportunity Pipeline Documentation

## Pipeline Stages

1. `New`: Newly discovered opportunity.
2. `Research`: Under qualification and contact research.
3. `Qualified`: Verified topical alignment and domain authority.
4. `Contacted`: Initial pitch sent to editor/publisher.
5. `Waiting`: Pending response.
6. `Negotiation`: Content terms or fee negotiation.
7. `Approved`: Content draft approved.
8. `Published`: Backlink live and verified.
9. `Rejected`: Pitch declined.
10. `Archived`: Opportunity archived.
