# Phase 25 CRM & Contact Database Documentation

## Features

- **Organization Management**: Tracks publisher organizations, domain authority, trust score, average response times, and past collaboration success.
- **Contact Management**: Stores contact names, roles, public contact emails, contact forms, social profiles, and language/geo preferences.
- **Relationship Lifecycle**: Transitions contacts through stages: `New`, `Contacted`, `Responsive`, `Partner`, and `Blacklisted`.
