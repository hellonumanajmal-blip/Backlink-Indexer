# Phase 25 Reporting Engine Documentation

## Report Types

- **Campaign Summary**: Comprehensive report on campaign health, tasks, and budget conversion.
- **Pipeline Status**: Distribution of opportunities across the 10 pipeline stages.
- **Relationship Report**: Publisher trust metrics and response benchmark logs.
- **Opportunity Report**: Top prioritized opportunities ranked by AI opportunity score.
- **Performance Summary**: Overall conversion rates and backlink acquisition ROI.

## Formats

- PDF Document (`.pdf`)
- CSV Data Export (`.csv`)
