## AI Executive Summary Engine

Separates measured quantitative metrics from AI-driven strategy:
- **Measured Metrics**: Indexing status, visibility scores, backlink totals.
- **AI Strategic Recommendations**: High-impact topical keywords, link reclamation targets, and prioritized action items.
