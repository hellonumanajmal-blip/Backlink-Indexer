# Backlink Gap Analysis Engine Documentation

## Overview
The `OpportunityEngine` categorizes backlink equity into four primary opportunity segments:

1. **Competitor-Only**: High-authority domains linking exclusively to competitors. High acquisition priority.
2. **Shared**: Domains linking to both customer and competitors. Opportunity to expand link coverage.
3. **Missing Opportunities**: High-authority domain patterns in target industry segment not yet acquired.
4. **Lost Opportunities**: Backlinks previously held by customer but recently dropped or lost to competitor outreach.

## Prioritization Matrix
- **Domain Authority >= 85**: Priority = High
- **Domain Authority 70-84**: Priority = Medium
- **Domain Authority < 70**: Priority = Low
