# Reporting & Export Module Documentation

## Overview
The reporting engine generates executive export summaries in PDF and CSV formats for C-suite and agency stakeholders.

## Available Report Types
- **Executive Summary**: High-level overview of visibility, benchmark score, and top gaps.
- **Competitor Benchmark**: Detailed breakdown per competitor domain.
- **Backlink Gap**: List of high-value link acquisition opportunities.
- **Anchor Analysis**: Distribution breakdown and over-optimization risk assessment.
- **Domain Quality**: Geographic, TLD, and trust score metrics.
- **Trend Report**: Historical velocity and growth curves.
- **Recommendation Report**: Actionable prioritization matrix.
